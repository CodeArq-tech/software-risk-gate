03A SMALL STEP IN YOUR WORKFLOW

Bring risk into
the pull request.

Add a workflow, define your repository's risk map, and let your team tune the gates to fit how you ship.

Get started on GitHub↗
⌁Open source · Runs in your workflow · Your rules, your repository
risk-gate.ymlCOPY
# .github/workflows/risk-gate.yml
name: Risk Gate
on:
  pull_request:
    types: [opened, synchronize, reopened]
permissions:
  contents: read
  pull-requests: read
  issues: write
jobs:
  risk-gate:
    runs-on: ubuntu-latest
    steps:
      - uses: actions/checkout@v4
        with:
          fetch-depth: 0
      - uses: CodeArq-tech/software-risk-gate@v0.1.9
        with:
          token: ${{ secrets.GITHUB_TOKEN }}
